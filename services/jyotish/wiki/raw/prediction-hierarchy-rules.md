# Prediction Hierarchy and Conflict Resolution Rules

## Master Hierarchy for Vedic Prediction

When multiple indicators conflict, follow this hierarchy from highest to lowest authority:

### Level 1: Birth Promise (Rashi Chart D-1)
The Rashi chart shows what is promised in life. If something is NOT promised in D-1, no Dasha or transit can deliver it.

**Rule: D-1 is the foundation. Nothing overrides what D-1 promises or denies.**

### Level 2: Navamsha Confirmation (D-9)
Navamsha confirms or modifies the D-1 promise.

**Rules:**
1. D-9 does NOT override D-1 — it confirms or adjusts
2. Strong in D-1 + Strong in D-9 → results delivered fully
3. Strong in D-1 + Weak in D-9 → results promised but partially delivered
4. Weak in D-1 + Strong in D-9 → planet performs above expectations, but limited
5. Exalted in D-1 + Debilitated in D-9 → FAILS to deliver (critical rule)
6. Debilitated in D-1 + Exalted in D-9 → partial recovery, delayed success
7. Vargottama (same sign in D-1 and D-9) → results are reliable and consistent throughout life

**Vargottama Special Rules:**
- Vargottama in exaltation sign → extremely powerful, excellent results
- Vargottama in own sign → strong, self-sufficient results
- Vargottama in friend's sign → good results
- Vargottama in DEBILITATION sign → Neecha Vargottama = VERY BAD, not good
- Vargottama in enemy sign → consistent but negative results

### Level 3: Dasha Activation
Dasha determines WHEN a promise manifests. Without dasha support, a promise stays latent.

**Rules:**
1. Mahadasha lord sets the overall period theme — cannot be overridden
2. Antardasha modulates within the Mahadasha theme
3. If Mahadasha doesn't indicate an event, Antardasha ALONE cannot deliver it
4. Exception: very strong Antardasha lord in Kendra with Rajayoga can partially activate
5. Pratyantar Dasha narrows timing to specific month

### Level 4: Transit Trigger
Transits trigger events that are already promised by D-1 and activated by Dasha.

**Rules:**
1. Transit alone NEVER creates an event not promised in D-1
2. Double Transit (Jupiter + Saturn) must support the relevant house
3. Transit through signs with high Ashtakavarga bindus → smoother activation
4. Transit through signs with low Ashtakavarga bindus → obstacles even for promised events

### Level 5: Divisional Chart Refinement
Relevant divisional charts add detail to specific life areas.

**Rules:**
1. D-10 for career details (but career must be shown in D-1 first)
2. D-7 for children (but children promise must exist in D-1)
3. D-4 for property, D-12 for parents, D-24 for education
4. Yogas in divisional charts are NOT interpreted like D-1 yogas
5. Divisional yogas only work when D-1 dasha combinations support them

## Conflict Resolution Framework

### When Parashari and Jaimini Disagree
1. If both agree → confidence 80%+, predict confidently
2. If one agrees, other neutral → confidence 60%, predict cautiously
3. If they contradict → DO NOT predict, wait for more transits
4. Give slight edge to Parashari for timing, Jaimini for nature of event

### When Dasha and Transit Disagree
1. **Dasha favorable + Transit unfavorable** → event happens but with delays/obstacles
2. **Dasha unfavorable + Transit favorable** → opportunities appear but cannot be fully utilized
3. **Both favorable** → event manifests smoothly and fully
4. **Both unfavorable** → event does not manifest; if forced, negative outcome

### When Rashi and Navamsha Disagree
1. **D-1 strong + D-9 weak** → promise exists but delivery is partial (60-70%)
2. **D-1 weak + D-9 strong** → limited promise but executes well within limits
3. **Both strong** → full manifestation
4. **Both weak** → minimal results; area of life struggles

### When Multiple Life Areas Conflict
Example: Career Dasha is good but Marriage Dasha is bad.
- Each life area has its own set of indicators
- A planet can be good for one area and bad for another
- Predict each area independently using its own house/lord/karaka system
- Only cross-reference when the same planet rules both areas

## The Triple Confirmation Rule

Before predicting any major life event, require confirmation from at least 3 of these 5:

1. **D-1 Promise** — relevant house lord/karaka well-placed
2. **Navamsha Confirmation** — D-9 supports the D-1 indication
3. **Dasha Activation** — running Mahadasha/Antardasha of relevant planet
4. **Double Transit** — Jupiter AND Saturn aspecting relevant house
5. **Divisional Chart** — relevant varga chart confirms

**3 out of 5 confirms → predict with moderate confidence (70%)**
**4 out of 5 confirms → predict with high confidence (85%)**
**5 out of 5 confirms → predict with very high confidence (95%)**

## Common Prediction Pitfalls

1. **Don't predict from single indicator** — even an exalted planet alone doesn't guarantee success
2. **Don't ignore Navamsha** — D-1 exalted but D-9 debilitated = disappointment
3. **Don't ignore timing** — a Rajayoga with no Dasha activation is just potential
4. **Don't predict death casually** — requires longevity classification + Maraka dasha + transit confirmation
5. **Don't confuse AL with Lagna** — worldly success (AL) vs true nature (Lagna) differ
