# Yoga Interpretation Rules

## Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas (5 Great Person Yogas)
Formed when Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, or Saturn is in own sign or exalted AND in a Kendra (1, 4, 7, 10) from Lagna.

### Ruchaka Yoga (Mars)
- **Formation:** Mars in Aries, Scorpio, or Capricorn in houses 1/4/7/10
- **Results:** Brave, strong physique, military/police career, leadership, property acquisition
- **Timing:** Manifests during Mars Mahadasha or Antardasha
- **Strength modifiers:** Stronger if Mars has high shadbala, in own nakshatra, or aspected by Jupiter

### Bhadra Yoga (Mercury)
- **Formation:** Mercury in Gemini or Virgo in houses 1/4/7/10
- **Results:** Intelligent, eloquent speaker, successful in business/writing/commerce
- **Timing:** Manifests during Mercury dasha
- **Strength modifiers:** Stronger if Mercury is not combust, not with malefics

### Hamsa Yoga (Jupiter)
- **Formation:** Jupiter in Sagittarius, Pisces, or Cancer in houses 1/4/7/10
- **Results:** Wise, religious, respected teacher, wealthy, blessed with good children
- **Timing:** Manifests during Jupiter dasha
- **Strength modifiers:** Most powerful of the five. Stronger when Jupiter also aspects other Kendras.

### Malavya Yoga (Venus)
- **Formation:** Venus in Taurus, Libra, or Pisces in houses 1/4/7/10
- **Results:** Beautiful, artistic, luxurious lifestyle, good marriage, vehicles
- **Timing:** Manifests during Venus dasha
- **Strength modifiers:** Stronger in 4th or 7th house. Weaker if Venus is combust.

### Sasa Yoga (Saturn)
- **Formation:** Saturn in Capricorn, Aquarius, or Libra in houses 1/4/7/10
- **Results:** Authority through hard work, political power, longevity, servants/subordinates
- **Timing:** Manifests during Saturn dasha, typically after age 36
- **Strength modifiers:** Stronger in 7th or 10th house. Saturn must not be retrograde for full effect.

## Wealth Yogas (Dhana Yogas)

### Lakshmi Yoga
- **Formation:** 9th lord strong in Kendra/Trikona, AND Venus in own/exalted sign
- **Results:** Great wealth, luxurious life, favored by goddess Lakshmi

### Dhana Yoga (General)
- **Formation:** Lords of 2nd, 5th, 9th, 11th connected by conjunction, aspect, or exchange
- **Results:** Wealth accumulation. Stronger when involving multiple trikona-kendra connections.
- **Rule:** More connections between wealth houses = greater wealth potential

### Gajakesari Yoga
- **Formation:** Jupiter in Kendra from Moon (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th from Moon)
- **Results:** Wisdom, wealth, fame, respected in community
- **Strength:** Weakened if Jupiter is debilitated, combust, or in enemy sign

## Raja Yogas (Power/Authority)

### General Raja Yoga
- **Formation:** Lord of Kendra (1/4/7/10) conjoins or aspects lord of Trikona (1/5/9)
- **Results:** Power, authority, leadership. Intensity depends on planets involved.
- **Rule:** The more Kendra-Trikona connections, the more powerful the Raja Yoga

### Dharma-Karmadhipati Yoga
- **Formation:** Lord of 9th conjoins or aspects lord of 10th
- **Results:** Career through righteous means, fame, authority, government favor
- **Most powerful:** When both lords are strong and in Kendra/Trikona

### Neecha Bhanga Raja Yoga
- **Formation:** A debilitated planet gets cancellation through specific conditions:
  1. Lord of the debilitation sign in Kendra from Lagna or Moon
  2. Lord of the exaltation sign in Kendra from Lagna or Moon
  3. Planet itself in Kendra
  4. The debilitated planet is aspected by its exaltation sign lord
- **Results:** Rise from humble beginnings to great heights. Often more powerful than regular exaltation.

### Viparita Raja Yoga
- **Formation:** Lords of 6th, 8th, 12th placed in other dusthanas (6th lord in 8th, 8th lord in 12th, etc.)
- **Results:** Gains through crises, enemy's loss becomes your gain, unexpected inheritance
- **Timing:** Activates during the dusthana lord's dasha

## Doshas (Afflictions)

### Manglik Dosha (Kuja Dosha)
- **Formation:** Mars in houses 1, 4, 7, 8, or 12 from Lagna, Moon, or Venus
- **Effects:** Delays in marriage, marital discord, spouse's health issues
- **Cancellation conditions:**
  - Mars in own sign (Aries/Scorpio) or exalted (Capricorn)
  - Mars aspected by Jupiter
  - Both partners are Manglik (mutual cancellation)
  - Mars in Leo, Aquarius, or specific nakshatras
  - After age 28, dosha weakens significantly

### Kala Sarpa Dosha
- **Formation:** All 7 planets (Sun through Saturn) on one side of Rahu-Ketu axis
- **Effects:** Sudden ups and downs, feeling of being trapped, karmic patterns
- **Partial Kala Sarpa:** When one planet is beyond the axis — effects are milder
- **Note:** PVR considers this overhyped. Many successful people have Kala Sarpa.

### Guru Chandala Yoga
- **Formation:** Jupiter conjoined with Rahu
- **Effects:** Corrupted wisdom, religious hypocrisy, unorthodox beliefs
- **Mitigation:** Jupiter in strong sign or aspected by its own sign lord

### Pitru Dosha
- **Formation:** Sun afflicted by malefics, or 9th house/lord afflicted
- **Effects:** Problems related to father, ancestral karma, lack of fortune
- **Remedies:** Pinda Daan, Shraddha rituals, Surya mantras

### Shrapit Yoga
- **Formation:** Saturn-Rahu conjunction
- **Effects:** Past life curses manifesting, unexplained obstacles, fear and anxiety
- **Mitigation:** Hanuman Chalisa, Saturn and Rahu remedies
