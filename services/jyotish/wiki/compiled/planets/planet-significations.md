# Planet Significations in Vedic Astrology

## Sun (Surya)
- **Nature:** Malefic, masculine, hot, dry
- **Signifies:** Soul, father, government, authority, self-confidence, vitality, health, bones, heart
- **Karakatva:** Atma Karaka (soul significator when highest degree)
- **Exaltation:** Aries 10 degrees | **Debilitation:** Libra 10 degrees
- **Own signs:** Leo
- **Friends:** Moon, Mars, Jupiter | **Enemies:** Saturn, Venus | **Neutral:** Mercury
- **Dasha period (Vimshottari):** 6 years
- **Directional strength (Dig Bala):** 10th house
- **Rules:** Authority, kingship, government positions, spine, right eye, father's health
- **Interpretation rule:** Strong Sun in Kendra/Trikona from Lagna = leadership, government favor. Weak Sun = ego issues, problems with authority, father's health problems. Sun in 10th house = career in government or authority. Sun-Saturn opposition = conflict with authority.

## Moon (Chandra)
- **Nature:** Benefic (when waxing), malefic (when waning), feminine, cold, moist
- **Signifies:** Mind, mother, emotions, public, water, liquids, travel, left eye, blood
- **Karakatva:** Mind significator
- **Exaltation:** Taurus 3 degrees | **Debilitation:** Scorpio 3 degrees
- **Own signs:** Cancer
- **Friends:** Sun, Mercury | **Enemies:** None | **Neutral:** Mars, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn
- **Dasha period (Vimshottari):** 10 years
- **Directional strength:** 4th house
- **Interpretation rule:** Moon's condition determines mental state. Moon in Kendra strong and aspected by Jupiter = stable mind, emotional strength. Moon in 6th/8th/12th or with malefics = mental disturbance, anxiety. Waxing Moon = more benefic influence. Moon-Rahu conjunction (Grahan Yoga) = confusion, obsessive thinking.

## Mars (Mangal/Kuja)
- **Nature:** Malefic, masculine, hot, dry
- **Signifies:** Energy, courage, brothers, land, blood, surgery, accidents, police, military
- **Exaltation:** Capricorn 28 degrees | **Debilitation:** Cancer 28 degrees
- **Own signs:** Aries, Scorpio
- **Friends:** Sun, Moon, Jupiter | **Enemies:** Mercury | **Neutral:** Venus, Saturn
- **Dasha period:** 7 years
- **Directional strength:** 10th house
- **Interpretation rule:** Mars in houses 1, 4, 7, 8, 12 from Lagna/Moon/Venus = Manglik Dosha (marriage issues). Mars in own sign or exalted = courage, property. Debilitated Mars = lack of drive, blood disorders. Mars-Saturn conjunction = accidents, surgery risk. Mars aspects 4th, 7th, 8th from itself.

## Mercury (Budha)
- **Nature:** Benefic when alone or with benefics, malefic with malefics. Neutral gender.
- **Signifies:** Intelligence, speech, commerce, writing, mathematics, skin, nervous system
- **Exaltation:** Virgo 15 degrees | **Debilitation:** Pisces 15 degrees
- **Own signs:** Gemini, Virgo
- **Friends:** Sun, Venus | **Enemies:** Moon | **Neutral:** Mars, Jupiter, Saturn
- **Dasha period:** 17 years
- **Directional strength:** 1st house
- **Interpretation rule:** Mercury with Sun (combustion within 14 degrees) = Budh-Aditya Yoga if within same sign but NOT too close (within 3 degrees = combust). Strong Mercury = intelligence, business success, communication skills. Mercury in 2nd house = sweet speech. Mercury-Rahu conjunction = sharp but deceptive intelligence.

## Jupiter (Guru/Brihaspati)
- **Nature:** Greatest benefic, masculine, warm, moist
- **Signifies:** Wisdom, dharma, children, husband (in female chart), wealth, expansion, liver, fat
- **Exaltation:** Cancer 5 degrees | **Debilitation:** Capricorn 5 degrees
- **Own signs:** Sagittarius, Pisces
- **Friends:** Sun, Moon, Mars | **Enemies:** Mercury, Venus | **Neutral:** Saturn
- **Dasha period:** 16 years
- **Directional strength:** 1st house
- **Interpretation rule:** Jupiter in Kendra = Hamsa Yoga (one of Pancha Mahapurusha). Jupiter aspects 5th, 7th, 9th from itself. Jupiter's aspect on any house protects and expands that house's significations. Jupiter in 5th = good children, intelligence. Jupiter in 9th = fortune, dharma, guru's blessings. Debilitated Jupiter = poor judgment, financial problems.

## Venus (Shukra)
- **Nature:** Greatest benefic after Jupiter, feminine, cold, moist
- **Signifies:** Wife (in male chart), marriage, luxury, beauty, arts, vehicles, kidneys, reproductive system
- **Exaltation:** Pisces 27 degrees | **Debilitation:** Virgo 27 degrees
- **Own signs:** Taurus, Libra
- **Friends:** Mercury, Saturn | **Enemies:** Sun, Moon | **Neutral:** Mars, Jupiter
- **Dasha period:** 20 years
- **Directional strength:** 4th house
- **Interpretation rule:** Venus in Kendra = Malavya Yoga (luxury, beauty). Venus in 7th = beautiful spouse, harmonious marriage. Venus-Mars conjunction = strong sexual desires. Venus combust = relationship difficulties. Venus in 12th = expenditure on luxury, foreign travel. Venus-Saturn conjunction = delayed marriage or marriage to older person.

## Saturn (Shani)
- **Nature:** Greatest malefic, neutral gender, cold, dry
- **Signifies:** Longevity, discipline, servants, chronic disease, delays, democracy, legs, teeth, old age
- **Exaltation:** Libra 20 degrees | **Debilitation:** Aries 20 degrees
- **Own signs:** Capricorn, Aquarius
- **Friends:** Mercury, Venus | **Enemies:** Sun, Moon, Mars | **Neutral:** Jupiter
- **Dasha period:** 19 years
- **Directional strength:** 7th house
- **Interpretation rule:** Saturn's transit through Moon sign and 2nd/12th from Moon = Sade Sati (7.5 year period of challenges). Saturn in Kendra = Sasa Yoga (authority through hard work). Saturn aspects 3rd, 7th, 10th from itself. Saturn delays but does not deny. Strong Saturn = discipline, longevity, success through perseverance. Saturn in 10th = career success after 36. Saturn-Rahu conjunction = fear, anxiety, hidden enemies.

## Rahu (North Node)
- **Nature:** Malefic, acts like Saturn but more obsessive and deceptive
- **Signifies:** Obsession, foreign things, technology, unconventional paths, paternal grandfather, smoke, poisons
- **Exaltation:** Taurus/Gemini (debated) | **Debilitation:** Scorpio/Sagittarius
- **Own sign:** Aquarius (some say Virgo)
- **Dasha period:** 18 years
- **Interpretation rule:** Rahu amplifies the house and sign it occupies. Rahu in 10th = sudden rise in career. Rahu with benefics = amplifies their good results. Rahu-Jupiter conjunction = Guru Chandala Yoga (corrupted wisdom, religious hypocrisy). Rahu in Kendra from lord of Kendra = Raja Yoga. Rahu acts as the dispositor's agent.

## Ketu (South Node)
- **Nature:** Malefic, acts like Mars but more spiritual and detached
- **Signifies:** Moksha, past lives, detachment, spiritual knowledge, maternal grandfather, surgery
- **Exaltation:** Scorpio/Sagittarius (debated) | **Debilitation:** Taurus/Gemini
- **Own sign:** Scorpio (some say Pisces)
- **Dasha period:** 7 years
- **Interpretation rule:** Ketu gives sudden results, separation. Ketu in 12th = moksha, spiritual liberation. Ketu with Jupiter = spiritual wisdom (Ganapati Yoga). Ketu in 2nd = harsh speech, family separation. Ketu in 7th = detachment from marriage. Ketu gives results of the house lord it conjoins. Ketu is headless — acts without thought.
