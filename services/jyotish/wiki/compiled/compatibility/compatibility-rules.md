# Marriage Compatibility (Kundali Milan) Rules

## Ashtakoota System (North Indian - 36 Points)

### 1. Varna Koota (1 point)
- Compares spiritual compatibility based on nakshatra varna (caste)
- 4 varnas: Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya, Shudra
- Boy's varna should be same or higher than girl's
- Score: 1 (match) or 0 (mismatch)

### 2. Vasiya/Vashya Koota (2 points)
- Measures mutual attraction and control
- Based on Moon sign animal compatibility
- Score: 0, 0.5, 1, or 2

### 3. Tara/Dina Koota (3 points)
- Measures health and well-being of couple
- Count nakshatras from boy's to girl's, divide by 9
- Remainders 3, 5, 7 are inauspicious
- Score: 0, 1.5, or 3

### 4. Yoni Koota (4 points)
- Sexual compatibility based on animal nature of nakshatras
- 14 animal types with friend/enemy relationships
- Score: 0-4 based on animal compatibility

### 5. Graha Maitri/Rasyadhipati (5 points)
- Mental compatibility based on Moon sign lords' friendship
- If both Moon sign lords are mutual friends = 5 points
- Friend + neutral = 4, both neutral = 3, etc.
- Score: 0-5

### 6. Gana Koota (6 points)
- Temperament compatibility
- 3 ganas: Deva (divine), Manushya (human), Rakshasa (demon)
- Deva-Deva, Manushya-Manushya, Rakshasa-Rakshasa = 6 points
- Deva-Manushya = 5, Manushya-Rakshasa = 1
- Deva-Rakshasa = 0 (worst mismatch)
- Score: 0, 1, 5, or 6

### 7. Bhakut/Rashi Koota (7 points)
- Most important koota — affects prosperity and progeny
- Based on relative positions of Moon signs
- Auspicious positions: 1-1, 1-7, 3-11, 4-10, 5-9, 6-8
- Inauspicious: 2-12, 6-8 (with exceptions based on sign lord)
- Score: 0 or 7

### 8. Naadi Koota (8 points)
- Most points — represents health and genetic compatibility
- 3 naadis: Aadi (Vata), Madhya (Pitta), Antya (Kapha)
- Same naadi = 0 points (genetic incompatibility risk)
- Different naadi = 8 points
- Score: 0 or 8

## Supplementary Checks

### Rajju (Important — not scored)
- If both nakshatras share the same Rajju (body part), it indicates misfortune
- 5 rajjus: Pada (feet), Kati (waist), Nabhi (navel), Kantha (neck), Siro (head)
- Siro Rajju = widowhood, Kantha = death-like separation
- Must pass for marriage to proceed

### Vedha (Obstruction)
- Certain nakshatra pairs create vedha (obstruction)
- If vedha exists, marriage not recommended regardless of score
- Example: Ashwini-Jyeshtha, Bharani-Anuradha, etc.

### Stree Dheerga
- Boy's nakshatra should be at least 9 nakshatras ahead of girl's
- Ensures boy's longevity is protected

### Mahendra
- Count boy's nakshatra from girl's — should be 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25
- Promotes progeny and family prosperity

## Scoring Interpretation
- **26-36:** Excellent match — highly recommended
- **21-25:** Very good match — recommended
- **18-20:** Good match — acceptable with minor remedies
- **15-17:** Average — proceed with caution, remedies needed
- **Below 15:** Not recommended — serious compatibility issues

## Beyond Koota Matching
- Koota matching is necessary but NOT sufficient
- Also check:
  1. Mars dosha (Manglik compatibility)
  2. 7th house analysis in both charts
  3. Venus condition in male chart, Jupiter in female chart
  4. 8th house (longevity) comparison
  5. Dasha compatibility — avoid both running dusthana dashas simultaneously
