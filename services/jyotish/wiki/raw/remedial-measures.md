# Remedial Measures in Vedic Astrology

## General Principles
- Remedies do not change planetary positions — they strengthen the native's ability to handle the karma
- Genuine faith and sincerity multiplies remedy effectiveness
- Mantra remedies require proper initiation for maximum effect
- Charity should be done on the specific planet's day
- Gemstones work through color therapy and are worn for BENEFIC planets only
- Never wear gemstone of a functional malefic planet

## Planet-Specific Remedies

### Sun Remedies
- **Mantra:** Om Suryaya Namaha (recite 7000 times in 40 days)
- **Charity day:** Sunday
- **Charity items:** Wheat, jaggery, copper, red cloth
- **Gemstone:** Ruby (Manikya) — only if Sun is Yogakaraka or benefic
- **Fasting:** Sunday fast
- **Worship:** Surya Namaskar at sunrise, Aditya Hridayam Stotra
- **When to apply:** Weak Sun, afflicted 1st/9th house, father's problems

### Moon Remedies
- **Mantra:** Om Chandraya Namaha (recite 11000 times in 40 days)
- **Charity day:** Monday
- **Charity items:** Rice, white cloth, silver, milk
- **Gemstone:** Pearl (Moti) — for weak Moon or mental health
- **Fasting:** Monday fast
- **Worship:** Chandra Darshan (viewing Moon on Shukla Dwitiya)
- **When to apply:** Weak Moon, mental distress, mother's health, emotional instability

### Mars Remedies
- **Mantra:** Om Mangalaya Namaha (recite 10000 times in 40 days)
- **Charity day:** Tuesday
- **Charity items:** Red lentils, red cloth, coral
- **Gemstone:** Red Coral (Moonga) — ONLY if Mars is benefic for the Lagna
- **Fasting:** Tuesday fast
- **Worship:** Hanuman Chalisa, Mangal Stotra
- **When to apply:** Manglik dosha, accidents, blood disorders, property disputes

### Mercury Remedies
- **Mantra:** Om Budhaya Namaha (recite 9000 times in 40 days)
- **Charity day:** Wednesday
- **Charity items:** Green moong dal, green cloth, emerald
- **Gemstone:** Emerald (Panna) — for weak or afflicted Mercury
- **Fasting:** Wednesday fast
- **Worship:** Vishnu Sahasranama
- **When to apply:** Speech problems, skin diseases, business losses, education issues

### Jupiter Remedies
- **Mantra:** Om Gurave Namaha (recite 19000 times in 40 days)
- **Charity day:** Thursday
- **Charity items:** Yellow cloth, turmeric, chana dal, gold
- **Gemstone:** Yellow Sapphire (Pukhraj) — for weak Jupiter or for blessings
- **Fasting:** Thursday fast
- **Worship:** Guru Stotram, Brihaspati Puja
- **When to apply:** Weak Jupiter, childlessness, lack of fortune, financial problems

### Venus Remedies
- **Mantra:** Om Shukraya Namaha (recite 16000 times in 40 days)
- **Charity day:** Friday
- **Charity items:** White items, rice, sugar, white cloth, silver
- **Gemstone:** Diamond (Heera) or White Sapphire — for benefic Venus
- **Fasting:** Friday fast
- **Worship:** Lakshmi Puja, Shukra Stotra
- **When to apply:** Marriage delays, relationship problems, luxury deficiency

### Saturn Remedies
- **Mantra:** Om Shanaishcharaya Namaha (recite 23000 times in 40 days)
- **Charity day:** Saturday
- **Charity items:** Black sesame, iron, blue/black cloth, mustard oil
- **Gemstone:** Blue Sapphire (Neelam) — CAUTION: test for 7 days first
- **Fasting:** Saturday fast
- **Worship:** Hanuman Chalisa (Saturday), Shani Stotra, Dasharatha Shani Stotra
- **When to apply:** Sade Sati, career delays, chronic disease, legal problems

### Rahu Remedies
- **Mantra:** Om Rahuve Namaha (recite 18000 times in 40 days)
- **Charity items:** Blue/black cloth, coconut, blanket to homeless
- **Gemstone:** Hessonite Garnet (Gomed) — with caution
- **Worship:** Durga Saptashati, Kalika worship
- **When to apply:** Rahu dasha issues, confusion, deception, fear

### Ketu Remedies
- **Mantra:** Om Ketave Namaha (recite 17000 times in 40 days)
- **Charity items:** Multi-colored cloth, sesame, dog food
- **Gemstone:** Cat's Eye (Lehsunia) — with caution
- **Worship:** Ganesha worship, Ketu Stotra
- **When to apply:** Ketu dasha issues, accidents, spiritual confusion, moksha path

## Universal Remedies
- **Maha Mrityunjaya Mantra:** For health and longevity (any planet affliction)
- **Gayatri Mantra:** Universal protection and wisdom
- **Navagraha Stotra:** For all nine planets collectively
- **Charity to Brahmins:** General karmic cleansing
- **Feeding animals:** Dogs (Ketu), cows (multiple planets), birds (Saturn)
- **Tree planting:** Reduces multiple afflictions
