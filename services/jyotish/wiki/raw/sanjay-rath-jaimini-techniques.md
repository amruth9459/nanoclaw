# Sanjay Rath Jaimini Prediction Techniques

## Upapada Lagna (UL) — Marriage Prediction

### Calculation
Upapada Lagna = Arudha Pada of the 12th house (A12)
- Count from 12th house lord to the sign it occupies
- Count the same distance from the 12th house
- Exception: if lord is in 1st or 7th from 12th house, count to 10th sign instead

### Key Rules
1. **UL sign and planets** → describe spouse's nature and appearance
2. **Benefics conjunct/aspecting UL** → spouse is good-looking and virtuous
3. **2nd from UL** → sustenance of marriage
   - Malefics in 2nd from UL → marriage breaks or native renounces world
   - Benefics in 2nd from UL → marriage is sustained
4. **Sun is NEVER malefic for UL** — Sun represents Dharma, marriage is Dharma
5. **Ketu causes marriage** — Ketu's dasha/transit activates marriage
6. **UL lord strong** → early marriage; weak → delayed marriage

### Marriage Timing via UL
- Dasha of sign containing UL or its lord
- Transit of Jupiter over UL or 7th from UL
- Ketu transit triggering UL axis

### Compatibility Check via UL
- Planets connected to native's UL should match partner's Lagna/trines
- Example: UL in Leo with strong Sun → partner should have strong Sun in Lagna or trines

## Arudha Lagna (AL) — Worldly Perception

### What AL Shows
- AL shows the world's perception of you (Maya/illusion)
- Lagna shows true self; AL shows perceived self
- Career success, fame, wealth are judged from AL
- Use AL for all worldly predictions; Lagna for true spiritual nature

### Key Arudha Prediction Rules
1. **Planets in/aspecting AL** → how the world sees you
2. **A10 (Arudha of 10th)** → shows type of work being done
3. **10th from AL** → professional reputation
4. **5th from AL** → reputation for intelligence
5. **2nd and 11th from AL** → sources of income and wealth
6. **A7 (Darapada)** → physical relationships, courtship
7. **UL (A12)** → long-term marriage partner

### AL vs Lagna for Judgment
- Judgment, balance, discretion → from 5th from Lagna (true self)
- Reputation, fame, social standing → from 5th from AL (perceived self)
- Competition and success → judged from AL
- Spiritual progress → judged from Lagna and Karakamsha

## Chara Karaka System (7 Karakas)

### Identification
Rank planets by longitude (degrees in sign), excluding Rahu/Ketu in 7-karaka scheme:
1. **Atma Karaka (AK)** — Highest degree — Soul significator
2. **Amatya Karaka (AmK)** — 2nd highest — Career/minister
3. **Bhratru Karaka (BK)** — 3rd — Siblings/courage
4. **Matru Karaka (MK)** — 4th — Mother/happiness
5. **Putra Karaka (PK)** — 5th — Children/intelligence
6. **Gnati Karaka (GK)** — 6th — Enemies/obstacles
7. **Dara Karaka (DK)** — Lowest degree — Spouse

### Career Prediction via AmK
- AmK planet type determines career field
- AmK in Kendra → strong career, government/leadership potential
- AmK in Dusthana → career struggles, working for others
- AmK conjunct/aspecting 10th house or A10 → prominent career

### Spouse via DK
- DK planet determines spouse's nature
- DK in strong dignity → quality spouse
- DK in Navamsha → confirms spouse characteristics
- DK conjunct AK → spouse is soulmate, deep karmic connection

## Narayana Dasha — Rashi-Based Timing

### Overview
- Rashi-based Dasha (not planet-based like Vimshottari)
- Starts from stronger of Lagna or 7th house
- Each sign runs for a period determined by its lord's position
- Best for timing events related to specific houses/signs

### Key Rules
1. Events manifest when Narayana Dasha activates the relevant sign
2. Argala (intervention) from other signs modifies results
3. Narayana + Vimshottari agreement = high confidence prediction
4. Narayana Dasha activates an entire sign — all planets in that sign deliver results

## Shoola Dasha — Death/Longevity Timing

### Purpose
Exclusively for timing death, health crises, and bodily events.

### Rules
1. Starts from stronger of Lagna or 7th house
2. Each Antardasha = 9 months; each Dasha = 9 years
3. **More malefics in a sign → stronger to kill** in Shoola Dasha
4. Death timing: Shoola Dasha of sign with most malefics + maraka connection
5. For father: starts from stronger of 3rd/9th
6. For mother: starts from stronger of 4th/10th

## Argala — Planetary Intervention

### What is Argala
"Argala means planetary/sign intervention" — the hidden influence of planets on houses.

### Types
1. **2nd from a sign** → Dhana Argala (wealth intervention)
2. **4th from a sign** → Sukha Argala (happiness intervention)
3. **11th from a sign** → Labha Argala (gains intervention)
4. **5th from a sign** → Putra Argala (children/intelligence intervention)

### Obstruction
- 3rd from sign → obstructs 2nd's Argala
- 10th from sign → obstructs 4th's Argala
- 12th from sign → obstructs 11th's Argala
- 9th from sign → obstructs 5th's Argala
- Obstruction only works if obstructing sign has fewer/weaker planets

### Argala in Marriage Timing
Chara Dasha rashi MD and AD should receive Argala from DK and/or their respective lords for marriage to occur.
