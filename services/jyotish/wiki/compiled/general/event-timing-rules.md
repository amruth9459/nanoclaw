# Event-Specific Timing Rules

## Marriage Timing

### Required Confirmations (need 3+)
1. **Dasha of 7th lord** or Venus or planet connected to 7th house
2. **Double Transit** — Jupiter AND Saturn aspect 7th house (from Lagna and Moon)
3. **Navamsha confirmation** — D-9 7th house and its lord well-placed
4. **Upapada Lagna** — Narayana/Chara Dasha activating UL sign
5. **Darakaraka (DK)** — DK's Dasha/Antardasha or DK receiving transit

### Navamsha Marriage Timing Rules
- D-9 Lagna lord in Kendra → early marriage
- D-9 Lagna lord in Trikona → slight delay
- D-9 Lagna lord in Dusthana + malefic influence → marriage after 30
- Venus in D-9 in 7th house → harmonious marriage
- 7th lord of D-1 in Kendra of D-9 → marriage promise confirmed

### Marriage Obstruction Indicators
- Saturn aspecting 7th house + 7th lord → delay (not denial)
- Rahu in 7th → unconventional marriage, inter-cultural
- Ketu in 7th → disinterest or spiritual spouse
- Mars in 7th (Manglik) → delay unless partner also Manglik
- 7th lord in 6th, 8th, or 12th → challenges after marriage

### Specific Dasha Combinations for Marriage
- Dasha of 7th lord → most likely period
- Dasha of planet in 7th house → triggers relationship
- Dasha of Venus → natural timing for marriage
- Antardasha of Ketu → Ketu causes marriage (Jaimini rule)
- Dasha of Navamsha Lagna lord → activates D-9 promise

## Career Timing

### Career Success Requirements (need 3+)
1. **10th lord strong** in D-1 and D-10
2. **Dasha of 10th lord** or Yogakaraka or Rajayoga planet
3. **Double Transit** — Jupiter and Saturn over 10th house or its lord
4. **Amatyakaraka (AmK)** — AmK well-placed and its Dasha running
5. **A10 (Arudha of 10th)** — planets connected to A10 activated

### Career Change/Promotion Timing
- Dasha of 10th lord → new career phase
- Saturn transit over 10th from Moon → restructuring
- Jupiter transit over 10th from Lagna → expansion, promotion
- Rajayoga Dasha + double transit on 10th → major career leap
- 6th lord Dasha → competition/service; 7th lord → business/partnership

### Career Planet Types
- Sun Dasha/connected to 10th → government, authority, politics
- Moon → public-facing, hospitality, nursing, travel
- Mars → military, engineering, surgery, police
- Mercury → commerce, writing, communication, IT
- Jupiter → education, finance, law, counseling
- Venus → arts, entertainment, luxury goods, beauty
- Saturn → labor, construction, agriculture, mining

## Wealth Timing

### Dhana Yoga Activation
1. 2nd lord + 11th lord connection (conjunction/aspect/exchange)
2. Must be activated during their Dasha/Antardasha
3. Jupiter transit over 2nd or 11th → wealth inflow period
4. Saturn transit over 11th → systematic wealth building
5. Rajayoga + Dhana Yoga in same period → sudden wealth jump

### Wealth Houses Hierarchy
- 2nd house → accumulated wealth, family money, savings
- 11th house → income, gains, desire fulfillment
- 9th house → fortune, luck, inheritance (father's wealth)
- 5th house → speculative gains, past-life merit
- 1st house → self-earned wealth

### Wealth Loss Indicators
- 12th lord Dasha → expenses exceed income
- Rahu in 2nd or 11th → wealth through questionable means, then loss
- 6th lord + 12th lord connected → debt trap
- Saturn transit over 2nd with low bindus → financial difficulties

## Health Crisis Timing

### Health Danger Periods
1. **Sade Sati** — Saturn over 12th, 1st, 2nd from Moon
2. **Maraka Dasha** — Dasha of 2nd or 7th lord
3. **8th lord Dasha** — transformation, surgery, chronic issues
4. **Badhaka lord Dasha** — obstruction planet's period
5. **Saturn transit over natal Sun** → vitality drops

### Longevity First Rule
Before timing health events:
1. Classify longevity (Balarishta/Alpayu/Madhyayu/Poornayu)
2. Major health crisis only in Maraka Dasha when longevity period aligns
3. Minor health issues can occur in 6th/8th lord Dasha without Maraka activation

### Body Part Indicators by House
- 1st → head, brain | 2nd → face, eyes, mouth
- 3rd → arms, hands, shoulders | 4th → chest, heart, lungs
- 5th → stomach, upper abdomen | 6th → intestines, kidneys
- 7th → reproductive, lower abdomen | 8th → chronic/hidden diseases
- 9th → thighs, hips | 10th → knees, bones
- 11th → calves, circulation | 12th → feet, sleep disorders

## Children Timing

### KN Rao's 4-Planet Method
All four must simultaneously activate:
1. Jupiter transit over/aspecting 5th house
2. Saturn transit over/aspecting 5th house or its lord
3. Mars transit triggers
4. Moon transit gives exact date

### Additional Requirements
- Dasha of 5th lord or planet in 5th
- D-7 (Saptamsha) confirmation
- Putra Karaka (PK) well-placed and activated
- Double Transit on 5th and 2nd houses

## Death Timing (Handle With Extreme Care)

### Prerequisites Before Considering Death
1. Must classify longevity group first
2. Native must be in age range of their longevity group
3. Maraka Dasha must be running
4. At least 3 independent confirmations required
5. NEVER predict death casually or from a single indicator

### Maraka Activation Requirements
1. Dasha/Antardasha of 2nd lord or 7th lord
2. Shoola Dasha activating sign with maximum malefics
3. Saturn transit over Maraka lord or 8th house
4. Rahu-Ketu transit over Lagna/7th axis
5. Ashtakavarga — transit through zero-bindu sign

### Death of Relations
- Spouse death → 1st lord Dasha (1st is 7th from 7th)
- Father → 4th lord Dasha (4th is Maraka from 9th) or check from 9th
- Mother → 11th lord Dasha (11th is Maraka from 4th) or check from 4th
- Siblings → 9th/3rd lord Dasha (Maraka from 3rd)
