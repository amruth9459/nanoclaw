# House Significations (Bhava) in Vedic Astrology

## Classification
- **Kendra (Angular):** Houses 1, 4, 7, 10 — pillars of the chart, strength-giving
- **Trikona (Trinal):** Houses 1, 5, 9 — most auspicious, dharma houses
- **Dusthana (Evil):** Houses 6, 8, 12 — suffering, obstacles, losses
- **Upachaya (Growth):** Houses 3, 6, 10, 11 — improve with age, malefics do well here
- **Maraka (Death-inflicting):** Houses 2, 7 — can cause death in appropriate dasha
- **Trishadaya:** Houses 3, 6, 11 — mildly malefic

## House 1 (Lagna/Ascendant)
- **Signifies:** Self, body, appearance, health, personality, beginning of life, head
- **Natural significator:** Sun
- **Interpretation:** Strongest house. Lord of 1st in Kendra/Trikona = strong personality. Malefics in 1st = health issues, difficult personality. Benefics in 1st = attractive appearance, good health. 1st lord in 6th/8th/12th = health challenges.

## House 2 (Dhana Bhava)
- **Signifies:** Wealth, family, speech, food, right eye, face, death (maraka)
- **Natural significator:** Jupiter
- **Interpretation:** 2nd lord strong = wealth accumulation. Malefics in 2nd = harsh speech, family conflicts. Jupiter in 2nd = sweet speech, wealth through knowledge. 2nd lord in 11th = good income. 2nd lord in 12th = expenditure exceeds income.

## House 3 (Sahaja Bhava)
- **Signifies:** Courage, younger siblings, communication, short travels, arms, ears, writing
- **Natural significator:** Mars
- **Interpretation:** Malefics in 3rd do well — give courage. Mars in 3rd = brave, adventurous. 3rd lord strong = good communication skills. 3rd house activation in dasha = travel, new initiatives. 3rd is house of effort (parakrama).

## House 4 (Sukha Bhava)
- **Signifies:** Mother, happiness, property, vehicles, education, chest, heart, homeland
- **Natural significator:** Moon
- **Interpretation:** 4th lord strong with benefics = domestic happiness, property. Moon in 4th = close to mother, emotional contentment. Malefics in 4th = disturbance in home life. 4th lord in 12th = living away from homeland. Saturn in 4th = delayed property acquisition.

## House 5 (Putra Bhava)
- **Signifies:** Children, intelligence, past life merit (purva punya), romance, speculation, stomach
- **Natural significator:** Jupiter
- **Interpretation:** Most auspicious trikona. 5th lord in Kendra = intelligent, children prosper. Jupiter aspecting 5th = blesses children. Rahu/Ketu in 5th = unusual children or delays. 5th lord in 8th = complications in childbirth. 5th house also governs mantras and devotion.

## House 6 (Ripu/Roga Bhava)
- **Signifies:** Enemies, disease, debt, service, maternal uncle, digestive system
- **Natural significator:** Mars, Saturn
- **Interpretation:** Dusthana but also Upachaya. Strong 6th lord = victory over enemies. Malefics in 6th = good (destroy enemies). Benefics in 6th = weakened. 6th lord in Kendra = chronic health issues. 6th-8th-12th axis = Viparita Raja Yoga when lords exchange.

## House 7 (Kalatra Bhava)
- **Signifies:** Spouse, marriage, partnerships, business, public dealings, maraka (death)
- **Natural significator:** Venus
- **Interpretation:** 7th lord strong and well-aspected = happy marriage. Venus in 7th for male = beautiful wife. Jupiter in 7th for female = good husband. 7th lord in 6th = conflicts in marriage. Multiple planets in 7th = multiple relationships. 7th lord in 12th = foreign spouse.

## House 8 (Ayur Bhava)
- **Signifies:** Longevity, death, transformation, inheritance, occult, chronic disease, reproductive organs
- **Natural significator:** Saturn
- **Interpretation:** Most feared dusthana. 8th lord strong = long life. Benefics in 8th = protected from sudden death. 8th house determines manner of death. 8th lord in 1st = health struggles. Jupiter in 8th = long life, interest in occult. 8th house also governs research and deep investigation.

## House 9 (Dharma/Bhagya Bhava)
- **Signifies:** Fortune, father, guru, religion, higher education, long travel, thighs, dharma
- **Natural significator:** Jupiter, Sun
- **Interpretation:** Most auspicious house. 9th lord strong = fortunate, religious, good father. Jupiter in 9th = Dharma Karmadhipati Yoga potential. 9th lord in 10th = career through dharma. 9th house activation = pilgrimage, higher learning, meeting guru.

## House 10 (Karma Bhava)
- **Signifies:** Career, profession, authority, fame, government, knees, father's longevity
- **Natural significator:** Sun, Saturn, Mercury
- **Interpretation:** Strongest Kendra. 10th lord strong = successful career. Sun in 10th = government service. Saturn in 10th = career success after hard work (after 36). 10th lord in 7th = career through partnerships. Multiple planets in 10th = public prominence.

## House 11 (Labha Bhava)
- **Signifies:** Gains, income, elder siblings, friends, desires fulfilled, left ear, ankles
- **Natural significator:** Jupiter
- **Interpretation:** House of fulfillment. 11th lord in Kendra = desires fulfilled. All planets do well in 11th. 11th lord strong = steady income. 11th house activation in dasha = gains, networking. 11th from any house = gains related to that house.

## House 12 (Vyaya Bhava)
- **Signifies:** Losses, expenditure, foreign lands, moksha, bed pleasures, feet, left eye, hospitals
- **Natural significator:** Saturn, Ketu
- **Interpretation:** Dusthana but also moksha house. 12th lord strong with spirituals = liberation. Benefics in 12th = good bed pleasures, peaceful sleep. 12th lord in 1st = expenditure on self. 12th house activation = foreign travel, hospitalization, or spiritual retreat. Jupiter in 12th = spending on charity.

## Key House Relationships
- **1-7 axis:** Self vs. Others (marriage, partnerships)
- **2-8 axis:** Wealth vs. Inheritance/Death (financial transformation)
- **3-9 axis:** Effort vs. Fortune (short vs. long distance)
- **4-10 axis:** Home vs. Career (private vs. public life)
- **5-11 axis:** Creation vs. Fulfillment (children/creativity vs. gains)
- **6-12 axis:** Service vs. Liberation (enemies/disease vs. moksha)
