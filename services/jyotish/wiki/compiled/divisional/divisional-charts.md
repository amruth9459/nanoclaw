# Divisional Charts (Varga) Interpretation

## Key Divisional Charts

### D-1 (Rasi) — Physical Body, General Life
- Base chart. All analysis begins here.
- Shows the physical manifestation of life events.

### D-9 (Navamsa) — Dharma, Marriage, Inner Strength
- **Most important divisional chart after D-1**
- Shows: Marriage quality, spouse characteristics, dharmic path, inner strength of planets
- Rule: D-1 shows potential, D-9 shows fulfillment
- Vargottama planets (same sign D-1 and D-9) = reliable strength
- D-9 Lagna = the dharmic direction of the soul
- 7th house of D-9 = spouse's true nature
- AK in D-9 (Karakamsha) = Jaimini's most important house

### D-10 (Dasamsa) — Career, Profession
- Shows: Type of career, professional success, public image
- 10th house of D-10 = nature of work
- Lagna lord of D-10 = how one approaches career
- Strong planets in D-10 = success in profession
- AmK (Amatya Karaka) placement in D-10 = career indicator

### D-7 (Saptamsa) — Children
- Shows: Number and quality of children
- 5th house of D-7 = first child
- Benefics in D-7 = children bring happiness
- PK (Putra Karaka) in D-7 = child-related events

### D-2 (Hora) — Wealth
- Simple chart: planets in Sun's Hora (Leo) = wealth through effort
- Planets in Moon's Hora (Cancer) = inherited or effortless wealth

### D-3 (Drekkana) — Siblings, Courage
- 3rd house matters viewed through this chart
- BK (Bhratru Karaka) placement here shows sibling relationships

### D-4 (Chaturthamsa) — Property, Fortune
- Shows: Fixed assets, property, vehicles
- 4th house and its lord in D-4 = property matters

### D-12 (Dwadasamsa) — Parents
- Shows: Father (9th house of D-12), Mother (4th house of D-12)
- Sun in D-12 = father's condition
- Moon in D-12 = mother's condition

### D-16 (Shodasamsa) — Vehicles, Luxury
- Shows: Vehicle ownership, luxury, conveyances
- 4th house of D-16 = vehicle matters

### D-20 (Vimsamsa) — Spiritual Life
- Shows: Spiritual practices, meditation, religious activities
- Jupiter in D-20 = spiritual wisdom
- Ketu in D-20 = moksha potential

### D-24 (Chaturvimsamsa) — Education, Learning
- Shows: Academic achievement, type of education
- 4th and 5th houses of D-24 = education quality
- Mercury and Jupiter in D-24 = academic success

### D-27 (Nakshatramsa/Bhamsa) — Strength, Vitality
- Shows: Physical and mental strength
- Planets strong here = robust constitution

### D-30 (Trimsamsa) — Misfortune, Evil
- Shows: Difficulties, chronic issues, hidden problems
- Used primarily for assessing misfortune timing

### D-40 (Khavedamsa) — Maternal Legacy
- Shows: Maternal inheritance and blessings

### D-45 (Akshavedamsa) — Paternal Legacy
- Shows: Paternal inheritance and blessings

### D-60 (Shashtiamsa) — Past Life Karma
- **Most important for past life analysis**
- Each D-60 portion has a specific deity/nature
- Planet in auspicious D-60 = good past life karma for that area
- Planet in inauspicious D-60 = karmic debt for that area
- D-60 lord's placement in D-1 = how past karma manifests

## Vimsopaka Strength System
- **Shadvarga (6 charts):** D-1, D-2, D-3, D-9, D-12, D-30 — basic strength
- **Sapthavarga (7 charts):** Above + D-7 — adds children
- **Dasavarga (10 charts):** Above + D-10, D-16, D-60 — comprehensive
- **Shodasavarga (16 charts):** All 16 main divisional charts — complete picture
- Score out of 20 points. Planet with 15+ = very strong. Below 5 = weak.

## Divisional Chart Reading Rules (PVR Method)
1. Never read a divisional chart in isolation — always cross-reference with D-1
2. The D-1 house lord's placement in the divisional chart shows the quality of that area
3. If a planet is strong in D-1 but weak in the relevant divisional = potential but poor execution
4. Planets in own sign or exalted in divisional charts = confirmed strength
5. Transit planets affecting the divisional chart lord's natal position trigger events
