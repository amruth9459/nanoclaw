# BV Raman Predictive Astrology Rules

## Chart Reading Methodology (Hindu Predictive Astrology)

### Step-by-Step Sequence
1. **Assess Lagna and its lord** — personality, health, overall direction
2. **Assess Sun and Moon strength** — vitality and emotional foundation
3. **Identify Yogakaraka** — the most beneficial planet for the chart
4. **Examine Kendras and Konas** — combinations between these create yogas
5. **Assess each Bhava and its lord** — specific life areas
6. **Check Dasha-Bhukti** — what period is running, what is activated
7. **Apply Ashtakavarga** — quantified transit strength
8. **Check divisional charts** — D-9 (marriage), D-10 (career), etc.
9. **Examine transits** — current planetary positions vs birth chart
10. **Synthesize** — combine all factors for prediction

## Maraka Rules (Death-Inflicting Planets)

### Primary Marakas
- **2nd house lord** — primary Maraka (death-inflicting planet)
- **7th house lord** — secondary Maraka
- **Planets occupying 2nd house** — become Maraka by association
- **Planets occupying 7th house** — become Maraka by association

### Death Timing
1. Death occurs in Dasha/Bhukti of Maraka planets when longevity period ends
2. Never predict death from single indicator — require 3+ confirming factors
3. Saturn transit over Maraka lords triggers danger periods
4. The timing depends on longevity group classification first

### Longevity Classification (Ayurdaya)
1. **Balarishta** (0-8 years) — infant mortality combinations
2. **Alpayu** (8-32 years) — short life
3. **Madhyayu** (32-75 years) — medium life (most common)
4. **Poornayu** (75-120 years) — full life

### Determining Longevity Group
- Lagna lord in Kendra + 8th lord in Kendra → Poornayu
- Lagna lord in Panaphara (2,5,8,11) + 8th lord in Panaphara → Madhyayu
- Lagna lord in Apoklima (3,6,9,12) + 8th lord in Apoklima → Alpayu
- Cross positions → average of indicated spans

## Ashtakavarga Prediction Rules

### Transit Scoring
- A planet transiting a sign with **4+ bindus** in its own Ashtakavarga → gives good results
- A planet transiting a sign with **0-3 bindus** → gives poor/harmful results
- **SAV (Sarvashtakavarga)** score of 25+ → sign is generally favorable for all transits
- SAV score below 25 → sign causes difficulties during any planet's transit

### Saturn Transit Ashtakavarga Rules
- Saturn in sign with 0-1 bindus in its BAV → severe difficulties, losses
- Saturn in sign with 2-3 bindus → moderate challenges
- Saturn in sign with 4+ bindus → Saturn gives good results (wealth, authority)
- Saturn transit through SAV 30+ sign → period of gains

### Jupiter Transit Rules
- Jupiter in sign with 4+ bindus → wisdom, fortune, expansion
- Jupiter in sign with 0-3 bindus → false hopes, wasted opportunities

## Yoga Activation Rules

### When Do Yogas Give Results
1. Yogas in D-1 activate only during relevant Dasha/Antardasha
2. **Panchamahapurusha Yoga** (Ruchaka/Bhadra/Hamsa/Malavya/Sasa) → activate during forming planet's Dasha
3. **Raja Yoga** → activates when either Kendra lord or Trikona lord's Dasha runs
4. Yogas in divisional charts are NOT interpreted independently — only relevant when D-1 Dasha combinations support them
5. A yoga with a weak (debilitated/combust) forming planet gives partial results

### Important Yogas

| Yoga | Formation | Result |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Gaja Kesari | Jupiter in Kendra from Moon | Wealth, fame, leadership |
| Dhana Yoga | 2nd + 11th lords connected | Wealth accumulation |
| Viparita Raja | 6th/8th/12th lords in each other's signs | Gains through adversity |
| Neecha Bhanga | Debilitated planet with cancellation factors | Rises from fall |
| Adhi Yoga | Benefics in 6th/7th/8th from Moon | Fame, authority |

## Specific Event Prediction Rules

### Marriage Indicators
- 7th lord well-placed and strong
- Venus (natural significator) unafflicted
- Jupiter's aspect on 7th house or its lord
- Double Transit over 7th house
- Dasha of 7th lord or Venus or planet connected to 7th

### Career Success Indicators
- 10th lord in Kendra or Trikona — authority and fame
- Sun well-placed — government connections
- Saturn strong — systematic, lasting career
- Rajayoga formed between Kendra and Trikona lords
- Dasha of 10th lord or Yogakaraka planet

### Wealth Indicators
- 2nd lord strong — family wealth, accumulated savings
- 11th lord strong — gains, income
- 9th lord strong — fortune, luck
- Jupiter strong — overall abundance
- Dhana Yoga active during relevant Dasha

### Health Indicators
- Lagna lord weak/afflicted — constitutional weakness
- 6th lord strong — resistance to disease
- 8th house afflicted — chronic conditions
- Malefics in Kendra from Lagna — health challenges
- Saturn transit over natal Sun or Lagna — health danger periods

## Gocharaphala (Transit Results)

### Saturn Transit Rules
- **Sade Sati** (7.5 year transit over Moon) — major life restructuring
- Saturn over 1st from Moon → health issues, mental stress
- Saturn over 4th from Moon → domestic unhappiness
- Saturn over 8th from Moon → danger to life, obstacles
- Saturn over 10th from Moon → career challenges/changes

### Jupiter Transit Rules
- Jupiter over 2nd, 5th, 7th, 9th, 11th from Moon → favorable
- Jupiter over 3rd, 6th, 8th, 12th from Moon → unfavorable
- Jupiter's beneficial transit partially counters Saturn's malefic transit
