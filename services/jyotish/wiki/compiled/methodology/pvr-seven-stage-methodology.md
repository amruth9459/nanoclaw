# PVR Narasimha Rao's 7-Stage Interpretation Pipeline

## Stage 1: Chart Construction and Verification
- Verify birth data accuracy (date, time, place, timezone)
- Compute Ayanamsa-corrected positions (Lahiri default)
- Check for borderline Lagna — if birth time is uncertain and Lagna is within 2 degrees of sign boundary, consider both Lagnas
- Verify basic chart properties match known life events (rectification check)
- Compute all required divisional charts (D-1 through D-60 as needed)

## Stage 2: Static Analysis — Strength Assessment
### Planet Strength
- Shadbala: 6-fold strength (Sthana, Dig, Kaala, Cheshta, Naisargika, Drik)
- Planets with Shadbala ratio > 1.0 are strong enough to deliver results
- Vimsopaka: 20-point strength across divisional charts
- Ashtakavarga: BAV (per planet) and SAV (total) — houses with SAV > 28 are strong

### House Strength
- Bhava Bala: strength of each house
- House lord's condition (placement, aspects, conjunctions)
- Planets in the house and their nature (benefic/malefic)
- Aspects on the house (Jupiter's aspect = protection, Saturn's = delay)

### Key Indicators
- Lagna lord's strength and placement = overall chart quality
- Moon's condition = mental and emotional state
- Sun's condition = soul purpose and authority
- 10th lord from Lagna, Moon, and Sun = career direction

## Stage 3: Navamsha (D-9) Cross-Check
- PVR's rule: "A promise in D-1 must be confirmed in D-9"
- D-9 is the chart of dharma and marriage
- Planet strong in D-1 but weak in D-9 = potential unfulfilled
- Planet weak in D-1 but strong in D-9 = late bloomer, spiritual compensation
- D-9 Lagna lord shows the dharmic direction of life
- Vargottama planets (same sign in D-1 and D-9) = strong and reliable
- Check D-9 for marriage timing and quality:
  - 7th lord of D-9
  - Venus (male chart) / Jupiter (female chart) in D-9
  - Upapada Lagna (UL) from D-1, its lord in D-9

## Stage 4: Karaka Identification (Jaimini System)
- Identify all 8 Chara Karakas based on highest degrees:
  1. Atma Karaka (AK) — planet with highest degree = soul's desire
  2. Amatya Karaka (AmK) — 2nd highest = career indicator
  3. Bhratru Karaka (BK) — 3rd = siblings
  4. Matru Karaka (MK) — 4th = mother, happiness
  5. Putra Karaka (PK) — 5th = children
  6. Gnati Karaka (GK) — 6th = obstacles, enemies
  7. Dara Karaka (DK) — 7th = spouse
  8. Pitru Karaka (PiK) — 8th = father (some systems use 7 karakas)

### Karaka-Based Analysis
- AK in D-9: Soul's spiritual journey. AK in moksha signs = spiritual life emphasis
- AmK in D-10: Career nature. AmK in fire signs = leadership career
- DK in D-9: Spouse characteristics. DK in benefic sign = good spouse
- Karakamsha (sign of AK in D-9): Most important Jaimini indicator

## Stage 5: Dasha Analysis — Timing
### Vimshottari Dasha (Primary)
- Current Mahadasha lord: What life theme is active?
- Current Antardasha lord: What specific events are unfolding?
- Upcoming dasha transitions: What changes are ahead?

### Cross-Reference with Other Dashas
- Narayana Dasha: External events, circumstances
- Chara Dasha: Jaimini perspective — use with Chara Karakas
- Kalachakra Dasha: For specific timing precision
- Yogini Dasha: Quick verification of major events
- Use minimum 3 dasha systems for important predictions

### Dasha Interpretation Steps
1. Determine Dasha lord's house ownership (from Lagna)
2. Determine Dasha lord's placement (which house)
3. Check Dasha lord's strength (Shadbala, dignity, aspects)
4. Check Dasha lord's relationship with Lagna lord
5. Check if Dasha lord participates in any yoga
6. Predict: Theme = ownership + placement; Quality = strength + yoga participation

## Stage 6: Transit Layer (Gochara)
### Saturn Transit (Sade Sati)
- When Saturn transits Moon sign and adjacent signs (12th, 1st, 2nd from Moon)
- 7.5 year period of challenges, transformation, karmic lessons
- Peak phase: Saturn over natal Moon (Ashtama Shani if from 8th)

### Jupiter Transit
- Jupiter's transit over natal Moon and key houses gives timing of opportunities
- Jupiter transits through a sign for approximately 1 year
- Jupiter in Kendra from Moon = favorable year

### Double Transit (KN Rao)
- Events manifest when BOTH Jupiter AND Saturn aspect the relevant house/lord
- Example: Marriage when Jupiter and Saturn both aspect 7th house or 7th lord
- This is the MOST reliable transit technique

### Rahu-Ketu Transit
- 18-month transit through each axis
- Rahu transiting over natal planets = amplification/obsession
- Eclipse on natal planet = significant karmic event within 6 months

## Stage 7: Synthesis and Prediction
### Convergence Rule (PVR's Core Principle)
- "Never predict based on a single factor"
- At least 3 independent indicators must agree for confident prediction
- Dasha + Transit + Divisional chart confirmation = reliable prediction

### Prediction Confidence Levels
- **High (>80%):** 4+ factors agree, Dasha + Double Transit + D-9 confirm
- **Medium (50-80%):** 2-3 factors agree, some contradicting indicators
- **Low (<50%):** Single indicator, no transit support, conflicting factors

### Event Timing Formula
1. Identify which Dasha/Antardasha period activates the relevant house
2. Confirm Jupiter-Saturn double transit aspects the house
3. Narrow to month: Faster planets (Mercury, Venus, Mars) transiting the house
4. Look for eclipses or lunation near the relevant axis

### Common Prediction Patterns
- **Marriage:** 7th lord dasha + Venus/Jupiter antar + double transit on 7th
- **Career change:** 10th lord dasha activation + Saturn transit + Jupiter aspect
- **Children:** 5th lord dasha + Jupiter transit on 5th + Saptamsa (D-7) confirmation
- **Health crisis:** 6th/8th lord dasha + Saturn/Rahu transit on Lagna or Moon
- **Property:** 4th lord dasha + Saturn aspect on 4th + Mars involvement
- **Foreign travel:** 12th lord dasha + Rahu transit on 12th/9th + 3rd/9th activation
- **Death:** 2nd/7th lord dasha + Saturn/Mars transit + weak Ashtakavarga in 1st/8th
