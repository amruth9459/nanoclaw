# Transit (Gochara) Interpretation Rules

## Saturn Transit
- **Transit cycle:** ~29.5 years through zodiac, ~2.5 years per sign
- **Sade Sati:** Saturn in 12th, 1st, 2nd from natal Moon (7.5 years total)
  - Phase 1 (12th from Moon): Financial stress, emotional heaviness, isolation
  - Phase 2 (over Moon): Peak intensity — mental stress, health issues, major life changes
  - Phase 3 (2nd from Moon): Financial restructuring, family adjustments
- **Ashtama Shani:** Saturn in 8th from Moon — sudden changes, chronic issues
- **Kantaka Shani:** Saturn in Kendra from Moon — career/relationship pressure
- **Saturn return (~29.5 years):** Major life restructuring, maturity milestone
- **Saturn in Upachaya (3, 6, 10, 11):** Generally positive — hard work brings results
- **Vedha (obstruction):** Saturn's good transit spoiled if another planet is in the vedha position

## Jupiter Transit
- **Transit cycle:** ~12 years, ~1 year per sign
- **Jupiter over natal Moon:** Favorable year — wisdom, expansion, opportunities
- **Jupiter in Kendra from Moon:** Generally favorable period
- **Jupiter in 2nd/5th/7th/9th/11th from Moon:** Good results per Ashtakavarga
- **Jupiter in signs with high BAV (4+):** Excellent results in that sign's affairs
- **Jupiter return (~12 years):** Renewal of fortune and philosophical direction

## Rahu-Ketu Transit
- **Transit cycle:** ~18 months per sign axis (always retrograde)
- **Rahu over natal Sun:** Identity crisis, ego inflation, father's health
- **Rahu over natal Moon:** Mental confusion, obsessive thinking, mother's health
- **Rahu over natal Jupiter:** Crisis of faith, unorthodox beliefs
- **Ketu over natal planets:** Detachment, loss, spiritual awakening related to that planet
- **Eclipse on natal planet:** Major karmic event within 3-6 months

## Mars Transit
- **Transit cycle:** ~45 days per sign (~18 months full cycle)
- **Mars over natal Lagna:** Energy boost, but also accidents if afflicted
- **Mars over natal Saturn:** Conflict, accidents, surgery risk
- **Mars return (~2 years):** New initiative cycle
- **Mars in 8th from Moon:** Avoid risky activities

## Ashtakavarga Transit Rules
1. When a planet transits a sign where it has high BAV (5-8 bindus) = excellent results
2. Transit through signs with low BAV (0-2 bindus) = poor results
3. SAV total of transit sign > 28 = generally favorable for all planets transiting there
4. SAV total < 25 = generally unfavorable

## Double Transit Rule (KN Rao)
- **Rule:** Both Jupiter AND Saturn must aspect the same house or its lord for events to manifest
- **Jupiter aspects:** 5th, 7th, 9th from its position (plus conjunction)
- **Saturn aspects:** 3rd, 7th, 10th from its position (plus conjunction)
- **Application:**
  - For marriage: Both must aspect 7th house or 7th lord
  - For career: Both must aspect 10th house or 10th lord
  - For children: Both must aspect 5th house or 5th lord
  - For property: Both must aspect 4th house or 4th lord
- **Critical:** Double transit provides the WHEN; Dasha provides the WHAT

## Transit Strength Hierarchy
1. Saturn transit (most impactful — slow, deep changes)
2. Jupiter transit (second most impactful — expansion, opportunities)
3. Rahu-Ketu transit (third — karmic events, obsessions)
4. Mars transit (fourth — energy, accidents, quick events)
5. Inner planet transits (Sun, Mercury, Venus) — minor triggers, timing fine-tuning
