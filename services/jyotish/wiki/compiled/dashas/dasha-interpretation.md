# Dasha Interpretation Rules

## Vimshottari Dasha — Primary Timing System

### General Principles (PVR Narasimha Rao)
1. The Mahadasha lord's house ownership and placement determine the THEME of the period
2. The Antardasha lord ACTIVATES specific events within the Mahadasha theme
3. Always check both from Lagna AND from Moon sign (Chandra Lagna)
4. The Dasha lord's relationship with Lagna lord determines whether results are favorable
5. Natural benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, Moon) give better results in their dashas unless they own dusthanas
6. Natural malefics (Saturn, Mars, Rahu, Sun) can give excellent results if they own Kendras/Trikonas

### Dasha Lord in Different Houses
- **1st house:** Focus on self, health, new beginnings, personality changes
- **2nd house:** Focus on wealth, family matters, speech, food habits
- **3rd house:** Focus on siblings, courage, short travel, communication projects
- **4th house:** Focus on mother, property, vehicles, education, domestic happiness
- **5th house:** Focus on children, intelligence, romance, speculation, spiritual practices
- **6th house:** Focus on health issues, enemies, debts, service, legal matters
- **7th house:** Focus on marriage, partnerships, business, public dealings
- **8th house:** Focus on transformation, inheritance, chronic issues, research, occult
- **9th house:** Focus on fortune, father, guru, higher education, long travel, dharma
- **10th house:** Focus on career, authority, fame, government dealings
- **11th house:** Focus on gains, income, elder siblings, desire fulfillment
- **12th house:** Focus on foreign travel, losses, moksha, hospitalization

### Dasha Lord Ownership Rules
- Lord of 1st, 5th, 9th (Trikona lords): Generally auspicious
- Lord of 4th, 7th, 10th (Kendra lords): Kendradhipati dosha — benefics become neutral, malefics become beneficial
- Lord of 3rd, 6th, 11th (Trishadaya lords): Mildly inauspicious
- Lord of 8th, 12th (Dusthana lords): Challenging unless Viparita Raja Yoga applies
- Lord of 2nd, 7th (Maraka lords): Can cause death/death-like experiences if other factors support

### Mahadasha-Antardasha Combinations
- **Trikona lord Maha + Kendra lord Antar:** Raja Yoga activation — best for career, authority
- **Kendra lord Maha + Trikona lord Antar:** Same Raja Yoga — slightly different expression
- **Dusthana lord Maha + Dusthana lord Antar:** Viparita Raja Yoga possible — unexpected gains through crisis
- **Maraka lord Maha + Maraka lord Antar:** Most dangerous for health/longevity
- **Yogakaraka Dasha:** When one planet owns both Kendra and Trikona (e.g., Saturn for Taurus/Libra Lagna, Mars for Cancer/Leo Lagna)

## Narayana Dasha — Rashi-Based Timing (Sanjay Rath Method)

### Interpretation Principles
1. The Dasha SIGN becomes the temporary Lagna
2. Read all houses from the Dasha sign as if it were the Ascendant
3. The lord of the Dasha sign and planets IN the sign determine events
4. Stronger for predicting external events and circumstances
5. Use with D-1 for general life, with D-10 for career, D-9 for dharma/marriage

### Key Rules
- Dasha of sign containing multiple planets = eventful period
- Dasha of empty sign = quiet period, focus on sign lord's activities
- Malefic planets in Dasha sign = challenges during that period
- Benefic planets in Dasha sign = favorable developments
- Dasha sign's lord in Kendra from it = strong period
- Dasha sign's lord in Dusthana from it = challenging period

## Chara Dasha (Jaimini)

### Interpretation Using Jaimini Karakas
1. Identify the Chara Karakas in the chart (AK, AmK, BK, MK, PK, GK, DK)
2. When Chara Dasha activates a sign containing a Karaka:
   - **Atma Karaka (AK):** Soul lessons, spiritual growth, major life themes
   - **Amatya Karaka (AmK):** Career, livelihood, professional identity
   - **Bhratru Karaka (BK):** Siblings, courage, initiatives
   - **Matru Karaka (MK):** Mother, happiness, property
   - **Putra Karaka (PK):** Children, intelligence, authority
   - **Gnati Karaka (GK):** Enemies, disease, obstacles (also cousins)
   - **Dara Karaka (DK):** Spouse, relationships, partnerships

## Double Transit (KN Rao Method)

### Principle
Events predicted by Dasha ONLY manifest when Jupiter AND Saturn BOTH aspect the relevant house by transit.

### Rules
1. Jupiter and Saturn must both aspect (by conjunction, 5th, 7th, or 9th aspect for Jupiter; 3rd, 7th, 10th for Saturn) the house of the event
2. OR aspect the house lord's natal position
3. This double transit must coincide with the Dasha/Antardasha activation
4. Example: Marriage happens when Dasha activates 7th lord AND Jupiter+Saturn both transit over 7th house or its lord

## PACDARES (KN Rao Framework)

For any event prediction, check these 8 factors:
- **P** — Planets (natural significators for the event)
- **A** — Aspects (planets aspecting the relevant house)
- **C** — Conjunctions (planets conjoined with house lord)
- **D** — Dasha/Antardasha (timing activation)
- **A** — Ashtakavarga (bindus in the house — 4+ is strong)
- **R** — Rajayoga (if Dasha lord participates in yoga)
- **E** — Exchange (if house lords exchange signs — powerful)
- **S** — Special aspects (Rahu/Ketu axis, retrograde effects)
